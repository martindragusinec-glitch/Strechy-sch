## Subsidies / financing (Nová zelená úsporám, NZÚ) — as of program rules effective 25 June 2026, verified 25 Aug 2026

- From 2026, NZÚ shifted from direct grants (for most applicants) to a **0%-interest loan** ("bezúročný úvěr") — state pays the interest, applicant repays only principal. Direct grants remain only for low-income/vulnerable households (**NZÚ Light**).
- **NZÚ (mainstream, 0% loan) — photovoltaics:**
  - 25,000 Kč per 1 kWp installed PV power
  - 15,000 Kč per 1 kWh of battery capacity
  - Max loan for PV: **400,000 Kč**
  - Interest rate: 0%
  - Loan term: 10–25 years, via participating banks/building societies
  - Minimum supported system size: 3 kWp
  - Example tiers shown: 3.85 kWp → 96,250 Kč; 4.40 kWp → 110,000 Kč; 4.95 kWp → 123,750 Kč; 5.50 kWp → 137,500 Kč; 6.05–9.90 kWp → 151,250–247,500 Kč (battery excluded from these example figures)
  - EV charging point also eligible for support
- **NZÚ Light (direct grant, for seniors / 3rd-degree disability pension / lower-income households):**
  - PV system + water heating: **120,000 Kč** direct grant, paid in advance to account
  - Solar thermal water heating with additional heating: **60,000 Kč**
  - Requires a "Renovační pas" (renovation passport) — now a mandatory supporting document for NZÚ Light applications and for partial-renovation loan applications; free for low-income households, discounted/market price otherwise
- Eligibility (general): must be an adult owner/builder of a house in CZ used for permanent residence (cottages need permanent residence registered there); house max 3 flats (apartment buildings need co-owners' consent)
- Current promotional discounts advertised on-site (time-limited, subject to change): "sleva 60 000 Kč" / "sleva 90 000 Kč" on PV, "sleva 79 900 Kč" / "sleva 57 500 Kč" / "sleva 32 500 Kč" on heat pumps; business financing: interest-free loan up to 60 mil. Kč for firms + up to 20% contribution to results

## Process / lead flow

- No explicit numbered "1-2-3" process graphic was found in the fetched pages. The actual "process" on-site is the **multi-step lead form** (Contact Form 7 + cf7-multi-step plugin), branded "Nezávazná poptávka":
  1. Choose interest: Fotovoltaika / Tepelné čerpadlo / Solární kolektor / Kombinace
  2. Answer 3–4 qualifying questions (building type, roof shape, heating source, household size, ownership — varies per product)
  3. Enter PSČ (postal code) → "Probíhá ověřování" (system searches for best ROI solution)
  4. Enter contact details (jméno, příjmení, e-mail, telefon) + GDPR consent checkbox → submit
  5. Promise: "Ozveme se vám do 24 hodin" (we'll contact you within 24 hours)
- Content pages (fotovoltaika, tepelná čerpadla) separately describe install prep, maintenance, financing options, etc. as FAQ-style accordions rather than a numbered pipeline.
